# Project records

Use these fields in the project's existing record. Do not create a new system when one already exists. Keep current observations dated and project specific.

## Brief

- Object being named; offering and core behavior/outcome.
- Industry/subsector, business model, positioning, differentiation, competitors/substitutes.
- Audience, user, buyer, trust context, category.
- Desired associations; exclusions; liked/disliked examples and why.
- Future scope; markets/languages.
- Preferred/excluded name types, ranked mix or explicit delegation; naming examples and reasons.
- Domain strategy and budget if relevant; required exact spelling.
- Intake fields marked answered, explicitly open/delegated, or unanswered.
- Trademark jurisdictions and intended goods/services.
- Hard constraints, preferences, unanswered questions, explicit assumptions.

## Territory register

| Territory | Research roots and sources | Depth | What emerged | Weakness | Keep / pivot / stop and why |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Record actual per-batch counts separately for roots investigated, raw constructions generated, names pitched, names rejected, and survivors screened. Mark unknown counts as unknown; never equate a long list with research depth. Preserve promising shelved roots and why they were set aside.

Record genuine semantic changes. Moving from weather to public institutions is a possible territory change; changing only a suffix is not.

## Candidate register

| Exact name | Pronunciation | Name type | Territory | Origin / construction and evidence | Intrinsic strength | Friction | Status / elimination reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Use raw, intrinsic pass, screening incomplete, screened candidate, finalist, rejected, or user selected. Track actual candidate counts only if recorded.

## External screening register

| Candidate | Check | Exact query / domain / mark | Source URL or database | Checked at | Market / jurisdiction / scope | Observed result | Limitation / next action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

For domains record registrar/registry and exact observed status, price/currency/term if visible. For trademarks distinguish exact, close spelling, phonetic, and related goods/services results. A no-hit query is not legal clearance.

## Finalist card

- Name and pronunciation.
- Type and research territory.
- Verified meaning/source or clearly labeled invented construction.
- Strategic contribution and what it communicates before explanation.
- Brand story and whether it works without the story.
- Pronunciation/spelling/recall assessment and whether humans actually tested it.
- Current domain, company, preliminary mark, language, and search/social findings, each with dates/sources or explicit "not checked."
- Strongest reason to use it; strongest reason to reject it.
- Recommendation and unresolved gates.

## Decision and continuation

Record the user's choice and date only when provided, reasons given, rejected alternatives, unfinished checks, and the concrete next action. The agent's recommendation is not the user's decision.

## Illustrative behavior

- "None of these feel distinctive": diagnose the prior roots and pivot to new territories rather than generating suffix variants.
- "It was available last month": keep the old observation dated and perform a fresh check before making a current availability claim.
- "Give me the best exact .com, but don't browse": explain that a checked recommendation cannot be completed. Present names only if exploratory ideas were explicitly requested; keep the exact .com requirement visibly unverified.
